## How IEEE style works in the report

1. **Numbering by first citation.**
   - Entries are numbered [1], [2], … in the order they are **first cited** in the text, not alphabetically.
   - The entries below are grouped by topic and have no numbers yet. Numbers are assigned while the report is written.
   - Only entries that are actually cited go into the final list.
2. **In-text citations.**
   - Single: "… mengikuti CRISP-DM [3]."
   - Several: "[3], [7]".
   - A range: "[3]–[5]".
   - With an author name: "Chawla dkk. [12] memperkenalkan …". Use *dkk.* for three or more authors and *dan* for two: "He dan Garcia [13]".
   - The bracket goes before the period: "… [3]."
3. **Reference list.**
   - Heading **Daftar Pustaka**.
   - Hanging number in square brackets.
   - Article titles in quotation marks with sentence case; journal and book titles in *italics*.
4. **Authors.** Initials before the surname: "N. V. Chawla". List all authors up to six. With more than six, write the first author + *et al.* The list keeps the English *et al.*; the Indonesian *dkk.* is for running text only.
5. **Journal names** are written in full, not as IEEE abbreviations, which avoids abbreviation errors. Keep this consistent.
6. **Datasets and web pages** end with "[Online]. Available: URL (diakses 9 Okt. 2026)". This is the date the project started using these sources.

### Patterns

- **Jurnal:** [n] A. B. Penulis, C. Penulis, and D. Penulis, "Judul artikel," *Nama Jurnal*, vol. x, no. y, pp. a–b, tahun, doi: ….
- **Prosiding:** [n] A. Penulis, "Judul makalah," in *Proc. Nama Konferensi*, tahun, pp. a–b, doi: ….
- **Buku:** [n] A. Penulis, *Judul Buku*, ed. ke-n. Kota, Negara: Penerbit, tahun.
- **Dataset / web:** [n] Penulis/Organisasi, "Judul," Penerbit. [Online]. Available: URL (diakses …).
